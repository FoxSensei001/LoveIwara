# Community guidelines / 社区规则

Use Issues and discussions for bug reports, feature requests, and technical questions.

- Be respectful and keep posts relevant to the project.
- Use sample data to reproduce problems. Remove personal information, private messages, and credentials from attachments.
- Share only material you have permission to publish. Keep posts and attachments suitable for a public technical forum.

Maintainers may remove unrelated or inappropriate posts.

Issue 和讨论区用于问题反馈、功能建议和技术交流。

- 尊重他人，交流内容与项目相关。
- 使用示例数据复现问题，移除附件中的个人信息、私信和凭据。
- 只分享有权公开的素材，正文和附件应适合公开技术社区。

维护者可移除无关或不适宜的内容。
